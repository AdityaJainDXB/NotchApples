Terms and refunds

Last updated 4 October 2026 (lifetime updates and tips added). Plain English, because this is a small project run by four high school developers.

What you buy

  • A Pro key ($1) or an Ultimate key ($5) unlocks that tier's features in Notch Apple, for life. It's a one-time payment: no subscription, renewal or expiry.
  • Every future update is included, 2.0 and beyond. Your key never expires.
  • An optional tip at checkout is a gift and doesn't change what you get.
  • Ultimate includes everything in Pro. Ultimate features marked "coming" arrive in later free updates.
  • A key works on up to 3 of your own Macs. Please don't share it publicly: shared keys may be turned off and reissued to the buyer.
  • Upgrading from Pro to Ultimate costs the $4 difference.

Paying

  • Customers in the UAE can pay by card in AED, through the payment provider Ziina: Pro is AED 4.40, Ultimate is AED 11, and upgrading from Pro to Ultimate is AED 6.60. The card fee, 3% + AED 1, is paid by the customer and shown before you pay. We never see your card details; Ziina handles them. Customers outside the UAE can also pay by card through Ziina, in US dollars at the dollar prices, with a higher card fee (5% + $0.30) that is paid by the customer and shown before you pay.
  • Everyone can pay in cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana or Litecoin), sent straight to the developer's wallet. Each order has a unique exact amount; that's how the payment is matched to you.
  • Network and exchange fees are paid by the sender. If the wrong amount arrives, open a request and it will be sorted out.
  • Prices don't include tax. You're responsible for any taxes that apply where you live.

Refunds

Refunds are handled case by case. Email notchapples.support@gmail.com with your transaction ID. If a refund is given, it's paid in the coin you used, to an address you choose (card payments are refunded to the same card through Ziina, minus the card fee Ziina keeps), and the key is turned off.

Privacy

  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • At checkout, your email is used only to send your key and for "Lost my key?". It's kept in plain text only until the payment arrives (at most 48 hours); after that only a salted one-way hash is stored, which is enough to recognise you when you ask for your key again.
  • Only if you tick "Email me a reminder" at checkout and then don't pay, your address is kept in plain text for at most nine days and you get at most three emails (about two, four and six days later; the last has a one-time 20% discount code). Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Paying, unsubscribing or the last email deletes the address from that list.
  • Activating a key sends the key and a one-way hash of your Mac, salted per key, to enforce the 3-Mac limit. About once a day, while a key is active, the app downloads the signed list of turned-off keys. Nothing about you is sent with it.
  • Only if you turn it on (Settings, off until you say yes), the app sends a random id made on your computer, the app version, the system version and your plan about once a day, so the developers can count users by version and system. It is not linked to your name, email or key, and the server keeps no IP address. If you write a feedback or problem note and press Send, the text you typed is sent too. Turning the setting off stops it; nothing else about you is sent.
  • This website has no ads or tracking either.

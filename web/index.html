Notch apple lives in the black cutout at the top of your screen. Click it or press ⌃⌥N and it opens over whatever you're doing. No windows to find. No app switching. On Macs without a notch, it's a small pill that works the same way.

Ask about anything on your screen.

A maths question, a Python error, a chart, a page in another language. Four steps, all from the notch.

  1. Capture

    Drag a box around it.

    Press ⌃⌥S in any app, even full screen. The display under your pointer freezes and you select a region, a single window, or the whole screen. The crop stays Retina-sharp.

  2. Choose

    It suggests what to do.

    Notch apple reads the text first and picks the likely action: Solve for maths, Code for an error, Translate for another language, Explain for a chart.

    SolveExplainExplain simplyHintSummarizeTranslateRewriteCodeAskExtract text, on your Mac
  3. Answer

    Read it as it's written.

    Answers stream in with readable maths instead of raw LaTeX, code blocks with Copy, and real tables. This one ran on the Mac itself with Ollama.

    ⌃⌥NOpen or close the notch⌘⇧VPaste an image or text⌘RRetry, with another provider if you switched
  4. Follow up

    Keep asking.

    "Why are there two answers?" "Make it simpler." The AI still sees your capture and its earlier answers. Stop, Retry and Edit work like you'd expect, and everything can be exported as Markdown.

Your AI, your Mac, your rules.

Pick the provider, keep the history, and always know what leaves your computer.

Every answer, saved locally.

Each task keeps its image, mode, question, answer and the model that wrote it. Search "quadratic" and pick up where you left off, or set history to delete itself after a day, a week or a month.

Questions.

Anything else? Open an issue.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. Notch apple shows a small pill at the top centre of the screen that works exactly the same way.

Is it really free?

Yes: the core app is free, and the code is source-available (you can read and build it, but not resell it or unlock the paid features without a key). AI uses free providers by default (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter's free models, or Ollama on your Mac). Pro ($1) and Ultimate ($5) are optional, one-time purchases.

How do I open and close it?

Click the notch, press ⌃⌥N in any app, or drag a file onto it. Press Esc or click elsewhere to close. You can also turn on "open on hover" in Settings → General.

Why does window snapping need Accessibility?

Moving other apps' windows is only possible through macOS Accessibility. Notch apple uses it only to read and set window positions.

What does capture need?

Screen Recording permission, asked the first time you press ⌃⌥S. macOS applies it after Notch apple restarts.

Which AI should I use?

Google Gemini is free and reads images. For complete privacy, install Ollama and run ollama pull gemma3:4b: answers are made on your Mac, offline. Settings → AI → Test connection checks your setup.

How do updates work?

Notch apple checks GitHub for the latest release and shows "Update available". Choose Update or Not now, or turn off automatic checks in Settings → Updates.

Can I make it smaller?

Turn off modules you don't need in Settings → Modules. Older, smaller builds are listed in the README.

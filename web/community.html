What people are asking for

Every few weeks Notch Apple asks, in the notch, for an idea, a problem or a word of praise. The people who build it read all of it and gather it here. Nothing on this page identifies anyone.

Nothing has been gathered yet. The first round comes a few weeks after this page appears.

Have something to say? Open the notch, then Settings → Help & Feedback (Mac) or Settings → Feedback and numbers (Windows). You can also open an issue on GitHub.

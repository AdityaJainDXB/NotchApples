Cookie policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. This page covers the website at adityajaindxb.github.io/NotchApples (and the pricing, support and terms pages on it). The Mac and Windows apps are covered by the privacy notes in the terms.

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies of its own, and has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts.
  • It uses your browser's local storage for a few things it needs to work. Nothing in it is sent anywhere by the site, and none of it identifies you.
  • One optional extra, asking GitHub for release info, stays off until you allow it.
  • You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, as easily as you made it.

What we use, by category

Strictly necessary (always on)

  • Your choice on this page. Local storage key na-consent: remembers whether you allowed the optional category, so we don't ask again. No expiry; clear it by clearing site data.
  • Checkout state on the pricing page: the order you have started (so a card payment that returns from the payment provider can be matched, and a crypto order survives a refresh). Stored only after you start an order; it holds an order id, not your name or card. Cleared when the order finishes.
  • Games on the home page demo. When you press Play on a demo game, it saves its own sound setting and best scores on your device (keys starting nightshift. or tetris.). You asked for the game, so this is part of providing it. Nothing is sent.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile security check. When you send a message through the support form or the help chat, a Cloudflare Turnstile check runs to keep bots out. It loads from challenges.cloudflare.com only at that moment, never on page load, and Cloudflare may set its own cookie or storage entry to do the check. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. If you would rather not use it, email us instead.

GitHub release info (optional, off by default)

  • The site shows the latest version from its own latest.json. Only if that is unavailable does it ask GitHub's API (api.github.com), and only if you allowed this category. That request shows GitHub your IP address. The answer is cached in local storage under keys starting gh: for an hour; withdrawing consent deletes them.

Analytics (not used)

  • We do not measure visits, clicks or scrolling on this site, and no analytics provider is loaded.

Marketing (not used)

  • No ads, no advertising networks, no retargeting, and no social-media widgets or embedded videos. If any of these are ever added, they will be a separate category, off until you opt in, and this page will say so first.

Your IP address and your device's time

  • Prices in your currency. When the pricing page asks our server for prices, Cloudflare passes along the country its network associates with your IP address, and we use it only to show AED or USD. We don't store the address or the country against you. If it is wrong or unknown you can pick the currency yourself on the pricing page.
  • Time in the demo. The clock and event times in the demo notch come from your own device's clock, in your browser. Nothing is sent for that.
  • Like any website, the hosts that serve this site (GitHub Pages) and our server (Cloudflare) see your IP address when a page or request is made. That is how the internet works and is covered by their own policies, not something we add.

Your choices

  • On this site: use Cookie settings in the footer, or the button above, to reject, allow or change the optional category at any time. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and nothing on the site is locked behind accepting.
  • In your browser: you can delete cookies and site data for this site at any time (in Safari: Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data; in Chrome: the padlock next to the address, then Site settings, Clear data; in Firefox: the padlock, then Clear cookies and site data). Doing so also deletes your saved choice here, so the banner will ask again.
  • Global Privacy Control: we leave the optional category off by default for everyone, which includes anyone sending that signal.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. For this website there is nothing tied to you to look up, because we keep no visitor records. For purchases, support messages and keys see the terms and privacy section. We do not sell or share personal information. Questions: notchapples.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change what the site stores or loads, we will update this page and ask again where a new choice is needed.

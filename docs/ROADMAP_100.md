# Roadmap: 100 ideas, ranked

Generated 6 October 2026 from the code as it stands (Mac 1.30.3, Windows 1.26.5 BETA). Every idea is something the apps do **not** do yet. Ideas that are impossible on these platforms are listed too, at the bottom of the ranking, with the reason.

## How the ranking works

`score = impact × reach × 10 ÷ (effort + 1)`

- **Impact (1–5):** how much it helps people who use the app daily.
- **Reach:** 1.5 if it ships on Mac and Windows, 1.2 for the server/admin side (it protects revenue), 1.0 for one platform.
- **Effort (1–5):** 1 is hours (a toggle or a small panel), 2 a small module, 3 a real module, 4 native or multi-part work, 5 depends on something outside our control.

Cheap, useful, two-platform ideas rise to the top. The top 50 are the build list; status is updated as each ships.

## Left out on purpose

- **Mirror iPhone notifications on the Mac:** iOS does not let an app read other apps' notifications.
- **Daily step goal:** needs HealthKit, which a non-sandboxed Mac app cannot read.
- **Google Calendar sign-in:** needs Google's OAuth verification (weeks, and a public privacy policy review). The iCal link works today.
- **Spotify like button, Hue direct control, Todoist sync:** each needs a third-party sign-in or pairing flow; worth doing after the cheaper wins.

The first entries in this list that were already half-built before the ranking (Claude usage tracker, Windows Claude Code dot, Windows games, Windows low-battery warning) are not counted here; they are in `docs/STATUS_AUDIT.md`.

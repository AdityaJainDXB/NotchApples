Each state is a different verb NotchAI can be doing, at 64 px here and 20 px in the pill. Pick one to drive the notch above.

Metal: metal-fx by Jakub Antalik, MIT licence; its WebGL engine is used unchanged (types stripped, modules joined) with a small plain-JavaScript wrapper in place of its React component. Orbs: thinking-orbs, same author and licence. Motion: transitions.dev by Jakub Antalik, MIT licence (panel reveal, text swap, icon swap, notification badge, menu dropdown, success check, card resize). The notch shape, tabs and copy are Notch Apple's. This is a web sketch, not the Mac app.

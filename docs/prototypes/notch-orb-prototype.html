The nine orb states

Each state is a different verb NotchAI can be doing, at 64 px here and 20 px in the pill. Pick one to drive the notch above.

Orbs: thinking-orbs by Jakub Antalik, MIT licence; its engine is used unchanged, with the TypeScript types stripped. Motion: transitions.dev by Jakub Antalik, MIT licence (panel reveal, text swap, icon swap, notification badge, menu dropdown, success check, card resize). The notch shape, tabs and copy are Notch Apple's. This is a web sketch, not the Mac app.

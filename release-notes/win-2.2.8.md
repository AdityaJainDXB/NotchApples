## Notch Apple for Windows 2.2.8 (BETA)

- Messenger: Connect Four, Rock Paper Scissors and Battleship in rooms and in Friends chats, and tic-tac-toe in Friends chats, from the Games menu next to the chat. Rock Paper Scissors picks and Battleship fleets are locked in with a hash first and checked at the end, so cheating shows. The notch grows to at least 800 by 600 while a board is showing.
- Plays against the Mac app (2.2.7 and 2.2.8) with the same messages. Windows cannot play Chess or Checkers yet, and its file limit is still 160 KB, so it ignores bigger files from Mac friends. Both are coming. Untested on real Windows hardware.

**Fixed**
- 🔝 **The notch sat below the top of the screen.** In 1.30.0 and 1.30.1 the notch could appear lower than the top edge (under the menu bar area) instead of flush with it. My full-screen hardening in 1.30.0 accidentally lowered the notch's window level, and macOS then pushed it down. The notch is back flush at the very top of the screen, and macOS can no longer nudge it below the menu bar. If you saw the notch, the volume gauge or other activities floating a little way down from the top, this is the fix.
- 🔊🔆 **Two volume and brightness pop-ups at once.** Pressing the volume or brightness keys could show Notch apple's gauge on top of the macOS pop-up, stacked over the top of the screen. That happened when "Hide the macOS volume and brightness pop-ups" was on but Notch apple didn't have the Accessibility permission, so macOS's pop-up couldn't be hidden. Now, until you allow Accessibility, only the macOS pop-up shows; once you do, only the notch gauge shows. (If you turn "Hide the macOS pop-ups" off yourself, you still get both.)

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🏠 **Choose your Home screen.** Settings → Modules & Layout → Home screen switches between **Classic** (the 1.33 page: today's weather, battery and your next events) and **Widgets** (the 1.34 cards). Classic is the default, and Widgets with nothing placed on Home shows Classic instead of an empty page.
- ↕️ **Rearrange your Home widgets.** A new Home widgets list in the same place moves each widget up or down, or takes it off Home (it then gets its own tab).

**Fixed**
- 🪟 **The first-run layout step now fits.** The "where should everything live" step in the welcome tour could be cut off in a small notch. Every step now scrolls when space is tight, and the layout list adapts to the width. It also lets you pick Classic or Widgets Home right there.
- 📤 **PairDrop file transfers now complete.** In 1.34.0 every file transfer failed partway; codes and chat worked. Fixed and covered by a real loopback transfer test that runs before each build.
- 🔗 **Connect to Claude** in the Claude usage tab (real session and weekly limits) is included from 1.34.0.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

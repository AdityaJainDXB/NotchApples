[security] Fixes the notch getting stuck open, and stops the hide shortcut from taking ⌘O away from every other app.
**Required bug-fix and security update.** Everyone moves to 2.0.6.

**Fixed**
- 🚪 **The notch can always be closed with the mouse again.** Pinning the notch kept it open for good, so clicking outside never closed it again, even after a restart. The pin now lasts only until you close the notch.
- ✖️ **A close button on every notch screen.** The lock, update, tour and what's-new screens hide the tab bar, so they had no close button. They now have one (they come back the next time the notch opens if they still need you).
- 🖱 **Clicking in Settings or another Notch apple window now counts as clicking outside** and closes the notch.
- ⌨️ **The hide shortcut is now ⌃⌥O instead of ⌘O.** As a global shortcut, ⌘O took "Open…" away from every app on your Mac. If you were on ⌘O you're moved over once; you can change it in Settings → Shortcuts & Hotkeys.
- 🗂 **The Non-Necessities tab no longer shows twice.** Rearranging tabs could save a tab twice; the order is cleaned up and can't repeat a tab any more.

Windows 1.32.1 BETA gets the same pin fix (it lets go when the notch closes) and the tab-order clean-up.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

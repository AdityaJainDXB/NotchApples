**Changed**
- ⌨️ **⌘J now opens and closes the notch, for everyone.** It replaces ⌘E and ⌃⌥N. If your notch shortcut was still one of those, it moves to ⌘J once; a shortcut you recorded yourself is left alone. Change it any time in Settings → Shortcuts & Hotkeys.
- Note that ⌘J no longer reaches other apps while this shortcut is on (it is Downloads in Safari and Chrome). If you need it back, record a different shortcut in Settings → Shortcuts & Hotkeys.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

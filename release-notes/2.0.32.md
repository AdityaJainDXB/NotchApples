**Changed**
- 📜 **A new licence.** Notch apple's code is now **source-available** under the Notch apple Source-Available License, with the copyright held by Aditya Jain: you can still read it, build it and help improve it, but you can't resell or redistribute it, or unlock the paid features without a key. Versions published before 9 October 2026 were MIT and stay MIT for anyone who has them. See `LICENSE`.

**New**
- 🔐 **More of the paid value now lives on the server,** where a modified copy of the app can't reach it. A real key on a registered device gets short-lived signed tokens from the licence server (renewed in the background, 72 hours each); content and rooms that need them are refused without one, and stop at once if a key is revoked.
- 🎹 **Four premium Klick sounds (Pro):** **Cherry MX Black**, **Gateron Ink**, **Alps** and **Buckling spring** (the loud IBM click). They aren't in the app: they download when you pick one (with your Pro key and a connection) and are kept on your Mac. Without a key the app falls back to a free sound.
- 📋 **Clipboard Link (Ultimate) now needs a pass from the licence server** to use the relay. The pass says "Ultimate" and nothing else, no key and no device, so the relay still learns nothing about you. Both devices need this update to link; older versions keep linking among themselves.

Details of what is and isn't protected: `docs/PROTECTING_PAID_FEATURES.md`.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

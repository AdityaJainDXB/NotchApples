**Printer Status**
- 🎥 **The printer's camera.** A new Camera card. On **P1 and A1 printers** (the A1 mini, for example) *Open camera* shows the live picture, over your own network: nothing goes through the internet or any server, and nothing is saved.
- For the **X1, P2 and H2** printers, which send H.264 video the app cannot play yet, the card offers *Copy video address* to open in VLC. Switch on *LAN Only Liveview* on the printer first (it does not turn the cloud features off).
- For printers added through your Bambu account, press *Find printers on my account* once more so the app learns each printer's access code; the address comes from the printer's own reports.
- Based on what the community has documented and tested against a simulated printer, not a real one: please tell us how it goes.

Windows 1.68.0 BETA has the same camera.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

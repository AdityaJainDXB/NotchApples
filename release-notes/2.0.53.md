**Fixed**
- ✈️ **No more crash when a flight starts.** The bigger fighters (F-15, F-22, Su-27) were dropped onto the runway from too high, because the start height ignored the model's size, so the landing was counted as a crash. Every aircraft now sits down cleanly. A helicopter stays put on its pad until you lift off.

**New in the Plane game**
- 🚜 **Ground crew.** Stopped on an apron: **1** refuel, **2** catering, **3** baggage, **4** pushback. Service trucks stand on the aprons, and airliners, business jets and turboprops start at the **gate** by default.
- 🛩 **Traffic.** Other aircraft cross the sky, with diamond markers and distances, a TRAFFIC readout and call-outs. A pass within 120 m is a Close call; a collision tears a wing off yours and sends the other aircraft down burning. You can turn it off in the Fly tab.
- ⚠ **Configuration errors.** Taking off or landing without flaps in an airliner, business jet or turboprop gives a "Config, flaps" call-out, and a landing without flaps costs points in Landing mode.

**Feedback**
- 💬 About every three to four weeks the open notch asks for an idea, a problem or praise. **Not now** skips it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.
- 🔢 **Count this Mac in the user numbers** (Settings → Help & Feedback; off until you say yes) sends a random id made on your Mac, the app version, the macOS version and your plan, about once a day. Nothing else.

Windows 1.56.0 BETA gets the same Plane fixes and the feedback card.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

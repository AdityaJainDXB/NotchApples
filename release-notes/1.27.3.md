**Fixed**
- 🫧 **The iPhone Duo open/close animation now works as intended.** Opening: the notch grows out with a soft overshoot while its contents come into focus out of a blur. Closing: the contents blur away quickly as the notch tucks back in, with no bounce. (Reduce Motion still gives a plain fade; turn Duo animations off in Settings → Notch or Modules for the plain ones.)

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

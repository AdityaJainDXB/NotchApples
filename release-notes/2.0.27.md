**New**
- 🗓 **Update at most once a week (optional).** Releases have been coming often, so you can now ask Notch apple to tell you about updates only once a week instead of for every release. **It is off by default.** After this update the notch opens on What's new with a switch for it, so you can turn it on or leave it off. You can change it any time in **Settings → Notch → Updates** and **Settings → Updates**.
- With it on, the notification, the **Update** button and the reminders in the notch come once a week for the newest version. Required security updates still appear straight away, and **Check now** always shows the latest release.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

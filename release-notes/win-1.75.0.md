**Battle: G-force control and a way back into the wave**

- Battle adds a default-on Over-G effects toggle and a self-destruct button (or X) that respawns you without resetting the wave or score.
- Open the Plane simulator full screen from its menu or with F11. Escape or F11 returns to the notch view.
- Enemy markers, the mouse aim ring and the red battlefield boundary warning remain clear and available.
- The Gemini model picker now removes duplicate model rows and ignores stale refresh results.

The Plane simulator and its Battle mode are shared with the Mac version.

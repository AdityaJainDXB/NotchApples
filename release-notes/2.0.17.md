**New**
- 💻 **Keep Awake (Lid Closed)** in Tools. Flip it on and your Mac keeps running with the lid shut, no external display needed. It asks for your password, turns itself off below 15% battery, shows a "Lid" mark beside the notch while active, warns you on battery, and is switched off again when Notch apple quits.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Skip for now**.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 📋 **The patch log lives in the notch.** After an update the notch opens on what changed, with a switch for each new optional feature and a **Got it** button that returns you to your tabs. The separate pop-up window is gone, and Settings → About → What's New opens the same card in the notch.
- 🔁 **Gemini switches model by itself.** If a Gemini request fails before answering (rate limit, timeout, a model that can't take the image), Notch apple retries on the next Gemini model, up to four in turn, and shows a short "switching Gemini module" notice in the chat and a small toast in the notch. A wrong API key, being offline or pressing Stop are not retried.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

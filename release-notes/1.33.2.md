**Fixed**
- 🔄 **"Can't write to …/AppTranslocation/…" when updating.** If you opened Notch apple straight from the DMG or the Downloads folder, macOS runs a temporary read-only copy and the update had nowhere to go. Updating now installs into Applications and opens from there, so the Update button works from any location. (Drag Notch apple into Applications once to avoid the temporary copy altogether.)

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

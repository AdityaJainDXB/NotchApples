**New**
- 📼 **Cassette mode, redone.** The tape is now large on the Now Playing tab with previous, play, next, progress and the rest of the controls beside it. A **Cassette mode in Now Playing** switch in Settings → Extras keeps it on or off until you change it (the tape button on the tab flips the same setting).

**Fixed**
- 📌 **A pinned notch stays open.** Clicking the desktop or another app, switching Spaces or swiping no longer closes it; only Esc, the close button or the shortcut do, so you can pin it and then drag a folder in.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

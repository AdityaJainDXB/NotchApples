**Fixed**
- ✈️ **The plane no longer keeps rolling to the right by itself.** Mouse steering read where the cursor *rested*, so a cursor left off-centre (often after clicking Take off) kept the plane banking. The mouse is now a stick that centres itself: move it to roll or pitch, stop and it springs back. With your hands off, the wings roll back to level on their own, and keys can no longer get "stuck" held down when the game loses focus.

**New**
- 💥 **Realistic damage.** Clip a tree, a building, a wind turbine or the ground with a wingtip and that wing tears off: the plane rolls hard towards the stump. Hit something with the nose or tail, or the ground too hard, and the plane breaks up into its wings, tail and fuselage, which tumble, bounce and burn.
- 🔧 **Failures.** The engine can quit (glide to a field) or run rough after a bird strike, with smoke trailing behind. Too fast and the wings come off; pull far too hard and one snaps. A hard landing collapses the undercarriage into a belly slide with sparks. A damage panel shows what still works. Turn failures off, rare or often in Controls.
- 🏔 **A much bigger world.** Real terrain with rolling hills, forests and a ring of snowy mountains, a river canyon to fly down, a strip on a plateau in the hills, a wind farm with turning blades, a radio mast, a town with streets and taller blocks, and a bridge to fly under. Land in the lake and you ditch.
- 🏅 **New challenges:** Canyon run, Mountain strip, Glider pilot, One-winged and Walked away.

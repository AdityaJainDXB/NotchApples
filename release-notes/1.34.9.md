**Required update.** Notch apple now keeps itself current.

- 🔄 **A background check every 15 minutes** (and after sleep) compares your version with the newest GitHub release. It runs even if you turned update notifications off. A copy that isn't the newest must update before it works; the notch shows only the **Update now** screen, and paid features and most shortcuts stay off until you do. Settings and the menu bar still open.
- 🛡 A release can include a `[security]` line in its notes; the update screen then says "Critical security update" with that message.
- The switch in Settings → Updates is now **Notify me about updates**. It only controls notifications.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🗣 **Realistic GPWS and callouts in the Plane game (airliners).** The 737, 747, 777 and the Airbus A320, A350 and A380 now speak, using your computer's own voice, and show the call on the panel. On take-off: **One hundred knots, V one, Rotate, Positive rate**. On the way down with the gear down: the radio-altimeter calls **One thousand, Five hundred, Approaching minimums, Minimums, One hundred, Fifty, Forty, Thirty, Twenty, Ten** and **Retard**. And the warnings: **Sink rate**, **Terrain, terrain** and **Pull up** (it looks ahead for rising ground), **Too low, gear**, **Too low, terrain**, **Too low, flaps**, **Glideslope** (below the 3° path to the runway) and **Bank angle**. There is a **GPWS voice** switch in Games → Plane → Controls, and Hoop Rush and Time Trial skip the warnings. The Cessna and Piper have none, like the real ones.

**Fixed**
- 🎮 **Games no longer get cut off in the notch.** On the Mac, the games list had grown to eight and pushed the Sound switch and the hint off the bottom; the list now scrolls and those stay in view. On Windows, 2048, Snake, Memory and Breakout were taller than the notch at the standard size; each game now scales to fit the space it has.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🪄 **Do It (Ultimate): tell the AI what to do, and it does it on your screen.** Open the new **Do It** tab, describe the task ("fill in this worksheet", "rename these files", "find the cheapest option"), and press **Start**. The AI looks at your screen and clicks, types, scrolls and presses keys, one step at a time, telling you what it's doing. A small bar at the bottom of the screen shows its progress with a **Stop** button, and **Ctrl+Option+Esc** stops it from any app. It **asks you first** before anything that sends, buys, deletes, posts or involves passwords or payment, and never types passwords or card numbers. Turn on **Confirm every step** to approve each action yourself. It stops after 40 steps. It needs a model that can see images (for example Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT) chosen in Settings → AI, plus **Screen Recording** and **Accessibility** for Notch apple. Your screenshots go to that AI provider while it works.
- 🎵 **Lyrics on the side of your screen.** In Now Playing, switch on **Lyrics on screen** (Pro) and the synced lyrics float along the left or right edge of your screen, Spotify-style: the line being sung is big and bright, the lines around it dimmer. It ignores the mouse, stays on top of your other apps and fades away when nothing is playing. Off by default.

Windows 1.42.0 BETA gets both.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

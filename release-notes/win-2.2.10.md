## Notch Apple for Windows 2.2.10 (BETA)

- Games: Rocket League, side-on car soccer with boost, a dash and boost pads, against computer players (Easy, Normal, Hard; 1 v 1 or 2 v 2) or a friend online with a room code. Plays against the Mac app's Rocket League. Not yet in Windows friend chats. Untested on real Windows hardware.
- Messenger: Chess and Checkers in rooms and Friends chats (the same rules and messages as the Mac, so Windows and Mac players can play each other), and files up to 2 MB in Friends chats and rooms (it was 160 KB), sent in paced pieces and put back together on the other side. Untested on real Windows hardware.

**New**
- 📊 **Claude usage animates the closed notch.** Every time your 5-hour or weekly use goes up by a percent, the closed notch pulses and shows how much is left of both ("5h 84% left" and "wk 92% left"). It turns yellow and then red as you near a limit.

**Fixed**
- 🔄 **Claude usage no longer goes stale.** It used to refresh only while the Home card was on screen, or every five minutes. It now reads your limits every minute in the background, again as soon as the Mac wakes, and retries within two minutes after a dropped connection. The Home card's lights and percentages follow at once.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

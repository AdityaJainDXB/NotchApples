**New in the Plane game**
- 🛫 **Cold and dark start** (Controls tab): the engine is off, a checklist is shown on screen (**T** hides it), and **I** starts the engine.
- 🎙 **Air traffic control.** Clearances for take-off, the approach and the landing, spoken and shown on screen (a switch in Controls turns the voice off).
- 🌙 **Time of day and visibility** in the weather panel: Dawn, Day, Dusk, Night (with stars), and Clear, Haze or Fog.

**Fixed**

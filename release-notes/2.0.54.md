**New in the Plane game**
- 🧭 **Autopilot.** In a fixed-wing aircraft press **K** in the air to hold your heading, height and speed. **, .** turn it 5 degrees, **[ ]** change the height by 50 m, and moving the stick (or **K**) hands the controls back.

**Fixed**
- 🛬 **Aprons are solid.** The aprons at the new airfields were treated as soft grass, so a nose wheel turning sharply on them could sink in and break (this affected every aircraft). They are paved ground like the runway now.
- ✈️ Other aircraft in the sky show their wheels when they are low.

Windows 1.57.0 BETA gets the same changes.

**A huge update.** Claude Code status dot, a rebuilt VPN page, AirDrop fixes and AirDrop for text, a pin button, two new games, and Live scores moved into Sports.

**New**
- 🟢 **Claude Code status dot.** A small dot beside the closed notch (and in the open notch's header): **green for 4 seconds** when a Claude Code task finishes, **yellow for 4 seconds** when it needs your input or approval, or finishes with warnings or errors, then it fades out. Settings → Notch Extras → Claude Code has **Add to Claude Code** (it adds two hooks to `~/.claude/settings.json` after saving a copy), **Copy the hooks**, and Try buttons. Any script can also use `open -g "notchapple://claude-code?status=done"` (or `attention`).
- 📌 **Pin button.** A pin in the notch header keeps the notch open (clicking elsewhere won't close it), so it stays put while you drag files in from Finder or the Desktop.
- 🍪 **Cookie Clicker.** Click the cookie, buy stronger clicks (each doubles a click) and auto-clickers (auto-clickers, grandmas, farms, mines, factories). Your progress is saved, and it keeps baking for up to an hour at half speed while you're away.
- 🏃 **Runner.** A fast arcade runner in the style of Geometry Dash: Space, ↑, W or a click jumps over spikes and blocks, and it speeds up the longer you last. Your best distance is kept.
- ✍️ **AirDrop text and code.** Share has a new **Text / code** tab: type or paste a string or snippet and send it by AirDrop, as plain text or as a file (`.txt`, `.md`, `.swift`, `.py`, `.js`, `.json` and more).
- ⚡ **A custom VPN of your own.** Add a VPN by hand (IKEv2, OpenVPN or WireGuard) with its address, username and password (and shared secret). Passwords stay in Notch apple's private file on your Mac.

**Improved**
- 🔌 **A new VPN page.** One big **power button** in the middle: tap to connect, tap again to disconnect, with a glowing ring while it works. The server picker is now a discreet menu on the right (your own VPNs, the free library, VPN Gate, with search). The "VPN on/off" badge is gone from the notch header, so the header stays clean; VPN status lives on the VPN page.
- 🏀 **Live scores are now in Sports.** Open the Sports tab and switch the right-hand card to **Live scores** (leagues, follow a team, and the score beside the notch). The old Live tab is now **Parcels & Flights**, and it is off by default.
- 🪟 **The notch stays put in full-screen apps**, including on Macs without a hardware notch (like the base M1 MacBook Air). The window is now re-asserted a few times as a full-screen transition settles, and can no longer be hidden along with the app.

**Fixed**
- 📥 **AirDrop drops.** Dropping files onto the AirDrop card now works anywhere on the card (not only on the small circle), reads files from Finder and the Desktop correctly, brings AirDrop's window forward, and tells you when AirDrop can't send (instead of doing nothing).

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- ✈️ **Plane, a 3D flight game in Games.** Pick a plane (Sparrow trainer, Mustang warbird, Falcon jet or Stunt Bipe), then fly:
  - **Hoop Rush:** 90 seconds, 1 point for every hoop you fly through.
  - **Time Trial:** 12 hoops in order around the valley, fastest time wins.
  - **Landing:** you're on final approach; touch down softly on the centre line and stop.
  - **Free Flight:** take off from the runway and explore the valley, the town, the lake and the mountains (and fly under the bridge).
- 🛩 **Flying that feels right:** lift and stalls from the angle of attack, drag, banked turns, flaps, brakes, take-offs and landings, with a gentle autotrim so it's never a chore. Chase, cockpit and orbit cameras, and engine sound.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards** for each challenge, and 14 **challenges** to unlock (loops, barrel rolls, a butter landing, the bridge…). Scores stay on this computer.
- 🎚 **Speaker balance in Now Playing:** slide left and all the sound goes to the left speaker, slide right for the right. It snaps back to the centre.
- 🎤 **A lyrics view like Spotify's:** click the song in Now Playing to see the cover, a progress bar and controls, with big synced lyrics that scroll with the song (lyrics are Pro).

Windows 1.33.0 BETA gets the same Plane game, speaker balance and lyrics view.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

This release includes everything from 1.32.1 to 1.32.4, which didn't build and were never published.

**New**
- 🌑 **⌥A turns the screen brightness to zero, and back.** Press Option+A to black out the built-in display, press it again to restore the level. Needs Accessibility; switch it off in Settings if you type "å".
- 🔔 **Update reminders.** While an update is waiting, every 4th or 5th time you open the notch it offers **Update** or **Skip for now**. A release marked required has no skip.
- ✨ **After updating, a window lists the new optional features** with a switch for each, once.

**Fixed**
- 🔓 **No more locked screen.** The "One required step" permission screen is gone and the notch always shows your tabs. Turn Accessibility on in Settings → Permissions for the volume and brightness gauge, ⌥A and window snapping.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**Fixed**
- 🫧 **The iPhone Duo open/close animation now works as intended:** the notch grows out with a soft overshoot while its contents come into focus out of a blur; closing blurs them away quickly as the notch tucks back in, with no bounce.

**Changed**
- 🧩 **Duo animations and full-screen persistence are in Settings → Modules** (at the top), as well as Settings → Notch. Both are on by default.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

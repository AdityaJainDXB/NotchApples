Notch.apple for Windows 1.68.0

- Printer Status: a Camera card. P1 and A1 printers (the A1 mini) show their live picture over your own network (nothing goes through the internet or a server, nothing is saved). X1, P2 and H2 printers send video this app cannot play yet: "Copy video address" opens in VLC (switch on LAN Only Liveview first).
- Press "Find printers on my account" once more so account printers learn their access code for the camera.
- Tested against a simulated printer, not a real one.

The installer is still named "Notch.apple".

**New**
- 🫧 **iPhone Duo animations** (on by default): the panel springs out of the notch, the highlight slides between tabs as one shape and pages scale into place, like the Dynamic Island. Settings → Notch → *Use iPhone Duo animations* turns them off. Reduce Motion always wins.
- 🧹 **Cache Cleaner** (Ultimate): scans app caches, Xcode derived data, logs and old temporary files, and clears what you tick in one click, with a running total of the space freed. Only your own folders, never Apple's caches.
- 🖥 **Keep the notch visible in full-screen apps** (Settings → Notch): for Macs without a hardware notch, like the base M1.
- The welcome tour's tab picker now offers Translator, Mac Stats and Cache Cleaner.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

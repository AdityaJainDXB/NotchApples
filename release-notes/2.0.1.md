**Update to this one.** Version numbers had gone up and down: a short-lived "2.0.0" went out and was withdrawn, and releases 1.34.x followed. This is **2.0.1**, higher than every version before it, so every copy of Notch apple, including anyone who installed 2.0.0, now sees it as the latest and updates normally.

It contains everything from 1.34.0 to 1.34.10: the new Home page, Claude usage lights with real limits, the algebra calculator, Non-Necessities, the rebuilt PairDrop with private chat, the guided tour, and your day (one thing today, countdowns, pinned notes, Up next) back on Home.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

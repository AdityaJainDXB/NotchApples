**New**
- ✈️ **Plane Sim, much more realistic.**
  - **Flying:** a proper stall with buffet and wing drop, spins in the light planes (recover with the stick forward and opposite rudder), ground effect and induced drag, wind, gusts and turbulence (stronger near the ground and over hills, with a wind arrow on the panel), true versus indicated airspeed and thinner air with height, and heavy planes that respond slowly. Pull too hard or go too fast and the wings overload: a g readout with a grey / red vignette, and wings that fail past the limit.
  - **Landing:** every wheel has its own spring and damper, so a hard landing bounces and the nose gear can collapse. You also get brakes per surface, tyre bursts, tail strikes and crosswind drift.
  - **Crashes like real life:** what happens depends on speed, angle and what you hit. A hard landing bends the gear; a belly landing slides along the runway with sparks; a rough field breaks the gear and shears the wings and tail off one by one; a fast hit into the ground gives a fireball, scattered debris and a burning wreck; ditching in water skips, then sinks. The wreck keeps its momentum, tumbles and slides until it stops, and a crash report says what went wrong ("Stalled at 80 m, impact 41 m/s, 63° nose-down", "Structural failure: 8.3 g", "Gear collapsed on a hard landing: 4.2 m/s sink") and whether it was survivable.
  - **A new world:** a much bigger island with sea all round, mountains up to 1.9 km with snow, a lake and rivers, beaches, farmland with hedges, forests of conifers and broadleaf trees, villages, a town and a city skyline, roads, pylons, wind turbines, a lighthouse and the bridge. The terrain gets detailed rock, scree and snow, a real sky with haze and a sun, clouds, and water with a sun glint and shore foam.
  - **A real airfield:** runway markings and numbers, edge, threshold and approach lights, PAPI lights that go red or white with your glide angle, taxiways, hangars, a tower, a terminal and windsocks that follow the wind.
  - **Better models:** all the planes were rebuilt with rounded fuselages, tapered and swept wings, engines with fans, flaps, ailerons and rudders that move, folding landing gear, and working nav, strobe, beacon and landing lights.
  - **Seven new modern aircraft (15 in all):** **Cirrus SR22**, **Pilatus PC-12**, **Cessna Citation**, **Embraer E175**, **Airbus A220**, **Boeing 787 Dreamliner** and the **F-35 Lightning** (afterburner, 9 g, retractable gear). The picker sorts them by kind. "Hangar tour" now counts all 15.
  - The fly-over cities (Dubai, London, Paris, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, Cairo, San Francisco and your own location) and the leaderboard work in the new world.

[required-update]
**Required update.** Everything from 1.34.0 to 1.34.6, plus a much tighter license check.

**Licenses**
- ⚡ **A suspended or revoked key now stops working within about 10 minutes online.** Notch apple checks the signed revoked list at launch, after sleep and every 10 minutes (it was every 6 hours in 1.34.6, once a day before that).
- ⏳ **A Mac that can't verify its license for 3 days pauses paid features** until it is online again (it was 30 days in 1.34.6). It comes back on its own the moment it can connect, and Settings → License says why it paused.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

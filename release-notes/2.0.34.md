**New**
- 🤖 **Notch apple AI, included with Pro and Ultimate.** In Settings → AI, choose **Notch apple AI (included)** and ask away: no API key to find or paste. Your request goes to Notch apple's server, which checks your key and uses the project's own AI account to answer. Pro gets 60 messages a day and Ultimate 200 (they reset at midnight UTC); your own keys (Gemini, Groq, Ollama and the rest) still work without a limit. Screenshots work too. It switches on once the server has its AI key, so you may see "isn't switched on yet" until then.
- A copy of the app without a real key can't use it: the server refuses it.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

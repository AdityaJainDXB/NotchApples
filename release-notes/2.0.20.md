**New**
- ⌨️ **Klick (Pro): your keyboard sounds like a mechanical one.** Every key you type, in any app, plays a switch sound: **Cream**, **Holy Panda**, **Blue**, **Red**, **Brown**, **Topre**, **Typewriter** (with a bell on Enter) or **Bubble**. Click a sound to choose it and hear it; the one you choose has its own **volume slider**. A big **on / off switch**, an optional key-up sound, and the space bar and Enter sound bigger, like real stabilised keys. Find it in the new Klick tab. It needs Accessibility to hear keys in other apps; only *that* a key was pressed is used, never what you type.

**Fixed**
- ✈️ **Plane controls work, and up now climbs.** Move the cursor **up to climb** and down to dive (and **↑** climbs too); Invert in Controls brings back the flight-stick way. The mouse steers inside a small ring in the middle of the view, so a short move is full stick and the cursor no longer runs off the notch. It takes control when you move the cursor into the middle, so a cursor left anywhere can't tip the plane. On the Mac, keys always reach the game even when the notch's focus is elsewhere.

Windows 1.37.0 BETA gets Klick and the same Plane fixes.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 📼 **Cassette mode on the Now Playing tab.** Press the tape button next to Open Player and the cover becomes a cassette: reels turn while it plays, the tape moves across as the song goes on, and previous, play and next sit on the front. Press it again to go back.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🧲 **Magnetic drop zones.** Drop targets now lean towards the file you are dragging, with a soft glow and a label for what happens on release: the Shelf, Convert, Share, Launcher, Wallpaper and the AI tab.
- ⏳ **Hold to clear, with a countdown Undo.** Clearing the whole Shelf or the finished to-dos is now press-and-hold, so it can't happen by accident, and the Undo that follows shows how long you have left.
- 🔎 **A model picker you can search.** In the AI tab, type to filter models, press Return to choose, and hover one to see what we know about it (provider, whether it can look at images, whether it runs on your Mac).
- 📼 **Cassette mode** for the music player (the tape icon on Home's Now playing card): reels turn while it plays and the tape moves across as the song goes on, with previous, play and next on the front.
- 🃏 **Two card games:** Blackjack (blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on 17, double down, play money) and Solitaire (Klondike, draw one).

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🔔 **Update reminders in the notch.** While a newer version is waiting, every 4th or 5th time you open the notch it shows an **Update** screen with **Skip for now**; skipping returns you to your tabs and the reminder comes back after another 4 or 5 opens. A release marked as required has no skip. It works with "Check for updates automatically" on.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

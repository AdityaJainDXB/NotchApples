**New**
- 🎾 **Tennis tab, on for everyone.** Every ATP and WTA match of the week, live, coming up and finished, with set-by-set scores, tiebreaks and who's serving.
- 👫 **Men, Women and Mixed** tabs, each with All, Singles and Doubles, and a chip for each tournament that week.
- 🔴 **Grand Slams in red:** the Australian Open, Roland Garros, Wimbledon and the US Open get a red banner, a 🏆 badge and a red glow.
- ⏪ **Earlier tournaments:** ‹ and › move a week at a time, and **Slams** jumps to any of the last four Grand Slams.
- 🏆 **ATP and WTA top 20** with points and movement (a saved list if ESPN's rankings can't be reached).
- ⭐ **Favourite players:** star them in the top 20, click one in a match, or type a name. Their matches come first, and their live score shows beside the closed notch while they play.

Windows 1.32.0 BETA has the same Tennis tab, with the live score on the pill.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

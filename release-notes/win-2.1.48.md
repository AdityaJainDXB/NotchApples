## Notch Apple for Windows 2.1.48 (BETA)

- Racing, formation lap fixed: your car no longer gets stuck in its grid box a few seconds in, and the grid now follows the road, so the back rows of a big field start on the track instead of in the sand behind it.
- Racing, Safety Car and Medical Car: you can drive them straight away, formation lap included. The Medical Car now follows the field round the formation lap and the first lap, a good way back, then goes into the pits (like F1); a car that falls behind it on the formation lap is disqualified. Driving for race control, you hear race control (who has crashed and how far ahead), not a race engineer, and a red pin on the mini map shows where the crash is.
- Racing, the radio stays open while you drive: press T (or Space in the Formula One car) and pick 1 to 7; T closes it. New: 7, retire the car, and a green line shows the way into the pit lane and to your box. The engineer and commentator now use the most natural voices on your computer instead of the robotic ones.
- Racing, pit stops are timed: arriving crooked in your box, or steering or pressing the gas while the crew works, adds time to the stop.
- Racing, after you hit a barrier a car check shows for a minute (tyres and damage, on a little drawing of the car), and your engineer reads it out.
- Racing, track designer: a picture of a circuit map now traces properly even with labels, DRS boxes, sector colours and corner numbers on it, at a real track size (15 m wide road, never longer than 8 km).

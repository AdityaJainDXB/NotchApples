**New**
- 🔔 **Limit alerts.** Once you have connected to Claude, a notification arrives when your real 5-hour session or weekly limit reaches 80%, and again at 95%. One alert per level per window. Switch it off in the Claude usage tab.
- ⏳ **Run-out forecast.** Under each limit, a line says when you would hit it at your current pace ("At this pace you hit it in 1h 20m, before it resets"). It only appears when that is before the reset.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

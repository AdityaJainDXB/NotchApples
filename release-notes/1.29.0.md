**New**
- 💻 **Lid Fold (off by default).** Fold your desktop like a closing laptop lid: it tilts, frosts and dims, then comes back. Turn it on in Settings → Lid Fold, then try **Preview**, the **timed demo**, or press **⌃⌥F** to fold and hold. On a MacBook with a lid-angle sensor you can also switch on **Follow the lid**, so it folds as you close the screen and unfolds as you reopen it. Macs without the sensor (for example the M1 MacBook Air) simply don't show that option working, and everything else still works.
- 🛟 **Always dismissible.** Click anywhere, press **Esc**, or press the hotkey and your desktop is back at once. Every fold except the lid-following one ends by itself (about 4 seconds for Preview, 12 for the demo, 30 for the hotkey), and any error, sleep, lock, display change or quit removes it.
- 🔒 **Private.** Lid Fold takes one still snapshot of each display to fold, never video or audio, and nothing leaves your Mac. Without Screen Recording it still previews, using a plain backdrop instead of your desktop.
- 🙏 **Acknowledgements** in Settings → About credit the open-source work Lid Fold builds on (Still, MIT, and LidAngleSensor, Apache 2.0), with the full licence texts.

**Under the hood**
- Lid Fold's logic lives in small tested packages (FoldCore, NotchKit) that CI builds and tests on every change. A shared notch arbiter decides who may use the notch, ready for upcoming features.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`. If macOS says Screen Recording is off after updating, open Settings → Lid Fold and choose **Relaunch**.

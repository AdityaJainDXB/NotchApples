**Battle mode fix and clarity update**

- Battle plane wings resist over-G, overspeed and wingtip scrapes; speed and G are limited. A full-speed nose-first crash is still fatal.
- Easy and Automatic warn and help pull up close to the ground; Pro gets a low-altitude warning.
- Enemy fighters and ground targets have clearer markers, edge arrows, smoke trails and wave objectives. Each new wave gets a short briefing.
- Fixed multiplayer wave progression getting stuck on wave 1. (Issue #22.)

The flight simulator is shared by the Mac and Windows versions.

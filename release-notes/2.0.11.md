**New**
- 🖱 **Fly with the mouse.** In the Plane game, move the cursor left or right to bank and turn, and down to pull the nose up (up pushes it down, like a stick). Keep it in the middle to fly straight. **W** adds throttle and **S** takes it away. The arrow keys still work, and a ring and dot on screen show where your cursor is steering.
- ⚙️ **A bigger Controls tab.** Games → Plane → Controls lists every key, has a **sensitivity** slider for the mouse, an **Invert Y** switch and a switch to turn mouse steering off.
- ✈️ **A new hangar:** the **Cessna 172** and **Piper PA-28** light aircraft, and the **Boeing 737**, **747** and **777** airliners. Airliners are heavy and turn slowly, so start your turns early and use flaps to land. (They replace the Sparrow, Mustang, Falcon and Stunt Bipe.)

- 📐 **The notch grows for the Plane game** (up to 960 × 600, never bigger than your screen) so the plane picker and challenges fit, and returns to your size when you leave it. (This was going to be 2.0.10, which was never published.)

Your Plane leaderboards and challenges are kept; old scores still show the plane they were flown in.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

## Notch Apple for Windows 2.1.50 (BETA)

- Racing, talk to your race engineer out loud: the first time you open Racing it asks whether to use the microphone. On the Mac app, open the radio (T, or Space in the Formula One car), say your question (“how are my tyres?”, “should I box?”, “what's the gap?”), and press again to send; the engineer answers with the live numbers.  Without the microphone the radio works with the number keys, as before. Before your first Formula One race there's a short radio check: send each of the six lines once. (Talking out loud is Mac only for now; on Windows the radio uses the number keys.)
- Racing, driving the Medical Car: race control tells you how the driver sounds on the way. At the crash, press F to get out, then pick the right question to ask the driver (only one tells you how they really are), stay with them for 5 to 30 seconds depending on how bad it is, and give your evaluation: red (serious), yellow or green (minor). Then you drive them back to the pits, and the Safety Car asks whether you're finished or going round one more lap. Each step scores.
- F1: the latest session's full timing (GitHub issue #35): each driver's tyre and how many laps it has done, gap, last and best laps, and pit stops, from OpenF1's free data.

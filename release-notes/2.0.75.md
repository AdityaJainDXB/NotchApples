**Battle, Plane and Games, easier to use**

- Battle now offers a default-on Over-G effects toggle. The new self-destruct button (or X) respawns you without resetting the wave or its score. The existing red boundary warning and limited mouse aim ring remain available.
- Open the Plane simulator full screen from its menu or with F11; Escape or F11 returns to the notch view.
- Games adds play-money Texas Hold’em against the computer, with a saved chip stack and standard seven-card hand ranking.
- Fixed the Claude Usage view clipping its contents in shorter windows: it scrolls within the available space.
- Hardened the Gemini model picker against duplicate provider results and overlapping refreshes.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

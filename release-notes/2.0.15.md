**New**
- 🏠 **Edit Home right in the notch.** Tap the sliders button on the Home page: move widgets up or down, make each one Small, Medium or Large, collapse one to a row, remove it, or add one back. No trip to Settings.

**Fixed**
- 📤 **AirDrop rebuilt.** Sending used to do nothing because AirDrop would not show its picker from the notch. It now opens from its own anchor window, shows what is happening, and if it still can't, **Open AirDrop window instead** opens Finder's AirDrop and selects your files to drag. The Shelf's AirDrop button uses the same route.
- 🖱️ **Scrolling up and down now does the same as left and right**, on the closed notch and on the tab bar.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Skip for now**.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**Fixed**
- 🛠 **The Plane game's Controls tab works again, and no more blank blue screen.** Opening the Controls tab in the Plane menu hit an error and, because the game remembers the last tab, every later launch showed only a blue screen. Fixed, and the menu now falls back to the first tab instead of going blank if a screen ever fails to draw.

**New**
- 🧭 **A proper cockpit for the Plane game.** Round **airspeed** and **altimeter** dials with real needles: the airspeed scale and its coloured arcs (white for flaps, green for normal flying, yellow for caution, a red never-exceed line) change with the aircraft, and the altimeter has three hands (100, 1,000 and 10,000 ft). There is also an engine gauge (**rpm** on the Cessna and Piper, **N1 %** on the airliners), a fuel level and the flaps position.
- 🔊 **Engines that sound like engines.** The light aircraft have a piston engine with a propeller beat that follows the rpm; the 737, 747 and 777 have a jet that spools up and down slowly, with a roar, a rumble and a whine (a 747 sounds fuller than a 737). Wind rises with speed, tyres rumble on the runway, and there is a **stall horn** and an **overspeed clacker**. Touchdown chirps and thumps, harder for a harder landing.
- ⚠️ **Tail strikes.** Raise the nose too far near the runway (about 14° in the Cessna, 13° in the Piper, 11° in the 737, 10° in the 747, 9.5° in the 777) and the tail scrapes: sparks, a scrape, and damage. A damaged tail means weaker pitch and rudder; a hard enough scrape breaks it off. The damage panel shows the tail.
- 🛩 **More like a flight sim.** Flaps now go down in **notches** (**F** down, **V** up) with each aircraft's own settings, a **flaps overspeed** warning, **fuel** that burns with the throttle (the engine stops when it runs out, with a low-fuel warning), and airliners call **ROTATE** at the right speed.

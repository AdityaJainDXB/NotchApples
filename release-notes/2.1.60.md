### Mac 2.1.60 · 25 October 2026 · 🏔️ Hills and elevation in the 3D racing view

**Racing: 3D hills — tracks now have elevation.** The 3D view draws every track with its ups and downs, car physics respond to gravity on slopes (uphill slows you down, downhill speeds you up), and the track editor can set a height on any point. Tracks with elevation can be shared with friends.

- Tracks with elevation can be saved and shared with a code, just like flat tracks.
- Car physics on slopes: gravity pulls the car downhill and resists uphill climbs, so you'll naturally slow on climbs and speed up on descents without touching the throttle.
- The 3D camera follows the car's height, so you see the road rise and fall as you drive.
- Barriers, grandstands, pit crew, and all scenery are drawn at the correct track height.

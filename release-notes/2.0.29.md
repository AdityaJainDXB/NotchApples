**New**
- 📡 **Flight Radar: every aircraft around you, live.** A new tab with a round radar centred on you (north up): each aircraft is a little plane pointing the way it is flying, coloured by height (green under 5,000 ft, yellow up to FL180, cyan above). Pick a range of 25, 50, 100 or 200 nm, click a plane (or a row in the list) for its callsign, type, registration, height, speed, heading and climb rate, and switch on **On the ground** to see aircraft at the airport. It uses your location (the same as your weather) rounded to about 1 km, asks adsb.lol's free public feed only while the tab is open, every 10 seconds, and stores nothing.
- ✈️ **Airbus in the Plane game:** the **A320**, **A350** and **A380** join the Cessna, Piper, 737, 747 and 777.
- 🛞 **Realistic landing gear.** The airliners now have a gear lever (**G**): raise it after take-off and lower it for landing. It takes a few seconds, shows DOWN, TRANSIT or UP on the panel (three green triangles when locked down), cuts drag when up, and the wheels fold away. Forget it and you get a "TOO LOW · GEAR" warning with a beeper; land with it up and you slide on your belly with the engines scraping. The Cessna and Piper have fixed wheels, like the real aircraft. The Landing challenge now starts with the gear up.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

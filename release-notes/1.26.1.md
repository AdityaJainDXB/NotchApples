**Changed**
- 🧹 The Ultimate **Cache Cleaner** tab is now the **Purge** tab. It opens the Purge app (a separate disk cleaner) from the notch, or links you to it if it isn't installed. Notch apple no longer scans or deletes anything itself.
- 👆 **Fixed:** a two-finger sideways swipe over the tab bar used to scroll the tabs and also switch to the next one. It now only scrolls. Settings → Notch → *Swipe on the tab bar switches tabs* brings the old behaviour back.

Everything from 1.26.0 is still here: iPhone Duo animations and keeping the notch visible in full-screen apps.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

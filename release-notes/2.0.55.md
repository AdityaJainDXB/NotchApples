**New in the Plane game**
- 📡 **ILS approaches.** Line up with any runway within 30 km (and about 17 degrees of its course) and an ILS box appears with the runway, the distance, and localiser and glideslope needles. Press **J** and the autopilot flies the approach, with tower calls, down to 45 m, where you take over. Gear and flaps stay yours.

**Fixed**
- 🛞 **Taxiways are wider** at the home airfield, so a big jet or a fighter no longer puts a wheel on the grass.
- 🌀 **No more tipping over** in fast ground turns: the nose wheel's steering is limited by speed.
- 🧭 The autopilot now holds its speed properly.

Windows 1.58.0 BETA gets the same changes.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- ⚔️ **Battle: a simpler, playable way to fight.** Air War is now **Battle**. Pick a vehicle, a battlefield and how you want to play, then start. No more 26-plane list or weather settings in the way.
- 🪖 **Drive a tank.** W/S drive, A/D turn, the mouse (or arrow keys) aims the turret and gun, Space or a click fires a big shell that explodes where it lands, and B fires the machine gun. Enemy tanks drive at you.
- 🗺️ **Three flat battlefields** made for fighting (the old mountain valley was too hard): **Green Plains**, **Desert Dunes** and **Island Fortress**. Each has a base at either end and a clear edge.
- 🤖 **Automatic, Easy or Pro.** Automatic flies the plane for you (it holds its height and speed; you point it and shoot, and bombs drop only when they would hit). Easy helps your aim. Pro is full control.
- 🤝 **Co-op or Fight**, and **enemies on or off**: switch the enemies off for relaxed target practice.
- The battle screen is cleaner: just a health bar, your weapons, a radar and a few markers.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New**
- 🗂 **Tidier Settings:** Permissions and Privacy are one entry, “Privacy & Permissions” (pick which one to open). Focus timer settings moved into Notch → Focus.
- 🐍 **Snake without the lag:** quick taps are queued in order instead of dropped, keys are read directly, the game ticks off the main thread and the board is one fast canvas.
- 🧱 **Breakout** and 🧠 **Memory:** two new games in the Games tab, each with a saved best score. One Sound switch covers all games.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

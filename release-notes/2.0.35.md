**New**
- 🖼 **Live wallpaper (Pro).** A new **Wallpaper** tab plays a video as your desktop wallpaper on every display and every desktop, behind your icons. **Add your own video** (up to **60 seconds**, up to **4K**, up to 800 MB; drop one on the tab or press Add), or get one from a small **free library of NASA clips** (public domain, downloaded from NASA only when you press Get): Lunar flyby, Goodnight Earth, Supermoon timelapse, Artemis II rollout, Airglow from the ISS and a Sun storm.
- 🔋 **Easy on your battery.** It pauses on battery power (switch that off if you like), in Low Power Mode, when the screen sleeps or locks, and whenever windows completely cover the wallpaper, because then nobody can see it. It is always silent.
- 🔒 **Lock screen:** macOS doesn't let apps play video there, so a still picture from your video becomes the system wallpaper (the lock screen and login window show it). Turning the live wallpaper off puts your old wallpaper back.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

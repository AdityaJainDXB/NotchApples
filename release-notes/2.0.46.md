**New**
- 🧲 **Drop zones are cards now.** The Shelf, Convert and Share drop zones show an icon tile, "Drop a file here" and "or click to browse". As you drag a file near, the card leans up to 10 points towards it, grows a little and glows with the title "Bring it closer", then "Let go to add it" when you are over it.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**Improved**
- 🔌 **Add your VPN app, nothing else.** The custom VPN form (server address, username, password, shared secret, config text) is gone. In the VPN tab, click **VPN app** and pick the one you already use: Proton VPN, NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Mullvad, Surfshark, WireGuard, Tunnelblick, Tailscale and others that are installed are listed for a one-click add, or choose any app. The big power button opens it, and turns green when your Mac actually has a VPN tunnel up. No passwords are stored by Notch apple.

**Fixed**
- 🔊 **The volume and brightness gauge didn't appear.** It needs the Accessibility permission, and without it nothing showed in the notch. That permission is now a required first step: until it is on, the open notch shows a short setup screen (what it's for, three steps, Open Accessibility Settings), the notch opens on it at launch, and the moment macOS reports the permission as granted the volume and brightness gauge starts, with no relaunch. If the permission is already switched on but the screen stays, there's a **Relaunch** button.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**Fixed**
- 🔓 **The notch is no longer locked behind a permission screen.** The "One required step" screen that replaced the tabs is gone, and the notch no longer opens by itself at launch. Accessibility is still used for the volume and brightness gauge, ⌥A and window snapping: turn it on in Settings → Permissions. Everything else works without it.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

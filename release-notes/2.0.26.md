**New**
- 🔲 **A border around the always-there buttons** (coffee, pin, settings, power, close), so they read as one fixed group.
- 🎛️ **Choose your top bar.** After installing or updating you pick which of those buttons to keep; change it any time in Settings → Notch. The power menu always stays.
- ⌨️ **⌘E opens and closes the notch, ⌘J hides and shows it.** Both can still be changed in Settings → Shortcuts & Hotkeys.
- 🧰 **Tools looks calmer.** Tools are now tidy tiles; tap one to open it and use it, tap again to put it away.

**Fixed**
- ⬆️ **Swipe up on the notch's top bar closes the notch again** (sideways still switches tabs).
- ⚙️ **Clicking Settings while it is open on another desktop brings it to the one you are on.**

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Skip for now**.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

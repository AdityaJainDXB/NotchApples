**New**
- 🎮 **Clear controls for every Plane mode.** Each flight starts with a "Controls" card that says what to press and what it does for that mode (hoops, time trial, landing, free flight, Air War). Press **?** or click the round **?** button any time to bring it back. The menu shows a short "How to play" under the mode buttons, and the Controls tab has the full list for each mode.
- 🖱️ **Air War is easier to fly.** Space or a click fires the guns, B drops bombs, N fires a missile, L fires flares, and there are clickable weapon buttons with ammo counts.

**Fixed**
- 💾 **The Plane game no longer resets when you close the notch or switch tab.** It is paused while you are away and put back exactly as it was (it is let go of after an hour).

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New in the Plane game**
- 🌍 **Fly with others.** Switch it on in the Fly tab and other Notch pilots who have it on appear in your sky, marked with their callsign, aircraft and distance. It sends only your callsign (your leaderboard name), aircraft and position every couple of seconds, with a random id and no account, and it is off until you turn it on. Other pilots cannot be hit. (It starts working once the server side is switched on.)
- 📋 **Step-by-step procedure.** For each phase (before take-off, climb-out, approach, landing roll) the next step is highlighted and every step ticks itself off. **T** hides it.
- 💡 **Night lights.** Runway, helipad and airfield lights, and your aircraft's own lights, glow properly at night.

Windows 1.60.0 BETA gets the same changes.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

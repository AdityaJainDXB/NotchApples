## Notch Apple for Windows 2.2.56 (BETA)

- New: a Utilities tab with PC stats, Shelf, World clock, Launcher and Convert on one screen. While it is on, those leave the tab bar and open from inside it. Turn it on in Settings → Tabs
- New: lock Messenger with Windows Hello or a 4 to 8 digit code. It is offered the first time Messenger opens, and you can change it any time in Settings → Security. Only Messenger is locked
- Removed: the VPN tab

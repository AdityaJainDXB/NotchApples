**Fixed**
- 🛠️ **Notch apple opens again.** Versions 2.0.17 and 2.0.18 crashed as soon as they started because of the new lid-closed Keep Awake tool. If the app won't open for you, download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, replacing the old copy (the in-app updater can't run while the app is crashing).

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Skip for now**.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

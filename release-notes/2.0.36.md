**New**
- 🗺 **A much bigger Plane world.** The map is about three times larger, with open plains, mountain ranges and a sea beyond the valley.
- 📍 **Fly somewhere real.** Pick a place on the Fly tab (Dubai, London, Paris, New York, Tokyo, Sydney, Cairo or San Francisco), press **My location**, or type a city or country. Each place puts its landmark beside the runway: the Burj Khalifa, Tower Bridge and Big Ben, the Eiffel Tower, the Empire State Building, Tokyo Tower, the Opera House and Harbour Bridge, the pyramids, the Golden Gate Bridge. Deserts for Dubai and Cairo.
- 🌍 **World leaderboard.** Tick the 🌍 box when you save a score and your name, plane and score join a shared board you can browse by challenge, all time or this week. Nothing else is sent, and you choose each time.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

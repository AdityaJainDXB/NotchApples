**New**
- ⌨️ **Press M in the Plane game to turn mouse steering off (and on again).** With the mouse off, fly with the keys only: arrow keys pitch and roll, **A** and **D** are the rudder, **W** and **S** are throttle. Your choice is remembered, and it is the same switch as in Games → Plane → Controls.
- The menu is now reached from the pause screen (**P**), since **M** has a new job.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

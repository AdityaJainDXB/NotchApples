**New**
- 🌐 **Play Battle with friends.** Open Battle, tap **Host a game** and share the 6-letter code, or **Join** with a friend's code. It works on the same Wi-Fi or far apart, and Mac and Windows can play together.
- 🤝 **Co-op or Fight.** In Co-op everyone is on one side against the computer. In Fight the players shoot each other (first to 10 kills wins), and if the enemies are on they shoot everyone. Everyone plays on the same battlefield against the same enemies.
- 🗳️ **Vote to change the match.** Co-op or Fight, enemies on or off, and the battlefield: the host can just choose in the lobby, and everyone else votes (press **U** during a battle). A change passes when more than half the players agree.
- 💥 If you are shot down or crash in a shared battle you come back after five seconds, and the battle goes on.
- You can see the other players' names, vehicles and health, and they appear on your radar.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

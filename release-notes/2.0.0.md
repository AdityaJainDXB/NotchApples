# HUGE UPDATE for Efficiency & Modular Design

Notch apple 2.0 is the biggest update yet: a new Home page, fewer tabs, a smarter Claude usage tracker, an algebra calculator, a PairDrop that works, and a guided tour that asks where you want everything.

**Home replaces Today**
- 🏠 A central **Home** page: **Devices**, **Notifications** and a **Quick Add** bar are always there, **Claude usage** is an optional card, and **Clipboard**, **Now Playing** and **Quick Notes** stay closed until you click them. Weather, your next event, battery, screenshot and recording are in the top row.

**Claude usage, simplified**
- 🚦 The 5-hour window and the week at once, each as a light: **green** on pace, **yellow** nearing the limit, **red** reached or burning fast. An arrow opens the detail (percentages, token counts, reset times).
- 🔔 A **5-second badge in the closed notch** shows the new colour and percentage when it changes, usage jumps by 10 points, or a limit resets.
- 📌 **Pin to Home** from the tab. Without a budget set, pacing is measured against your busiest window and week.

**Tools**
- ➗ A new **algebra calculator**: multi-line steps with variables and functions, equations (`2x + 3 = 11`), quadratics with exact or complex roots, higher degrees, systems of linear equations, `solve(a x + b = c, x)`, `expand`, `factor`, `diff`.
- 🌍 The seven-language **Translator** now lives inside Tools, and Tools has tidier, even spacing.

**One tab for the rest**
- 🗂 **Non-Necessities** merges Focus, World Clock, Audio, Snippets, Shortcuts, Timers, Plugins, Voice Notes, Screen Time and Smart Home into a single tab.
- ⚙️ **Settings → Modules & Layout** lets any feature live on Home (open or click-to-open), as its own tab, inside Non-Necessities, or be switched off.

**PairDrop**
- 📤 **Rebuilt transfers:** any file size (streamed, not held in memory), folders (zipped), a progress bar, "Sent" only when the other device has saved the file, and only five wrong codes per minute before it locks.
- 💬 **Private Chat** between two devices on the same Wi-Fi, and a **name you choose** (edit it in PairDrop).
- Older versions can still send you files.

**A guided tour**
- 🧭 After updating, the notch opens a **compulsory walkthrough**, one step per change with things to try (a live calculator, a notch badge preview, your PairDrop name, a full-screen test), ending with the layout chooser. Replay it from Settings → About.

**Please note**
- Existing features keep working: whatever you had on stays on, in its new place. Home is now the first tab; the old "Home" dashboard is called **Dashboard**.
- The Windows app is not part of this release.

**Updates**
Notch apple tells you when a release like this one is out: a notification, an **Update** button in the notch and a card in Settings → Updates. Choose **Update** or **Not now**, or turn automatic checks off.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

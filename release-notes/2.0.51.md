**Fixes and new**
- 🔄 **Updates work again.** "Couldn't reach GitHub" was GitHub's limit of 60 anonymous requests an hour per address, which a shared network uses up. Update checks now read a small file from the website first, then GitHub's release feed, and only then the API.
- 🚁 **Helicopters fly properly.** The stick leans the helicopter and it settles at that lean (right goes right, up goes forward); the collective (W / S) stays where you leave it; the rotor damps vertical motion so a steady collective gives a steady climb; the tail rotor turns at a calm rate; and the blades are drawn flat in the rotor disc.
- 🟩 **Helipads.** Helicopters start on a helipad (at the airfield, and on the mountain plateau). Landing mode asks for the H. New challenges: Hover steady, On the H, Top of the world.
- ⛰ **Mountain strip.** Landing and stopping there now counts however you got there.
- 🖨 **Printer Status.** The Bambu tab is renamed, its settings are laid out properly, and the steps to connect a printer are listed.

Windows 1.54.0 BETA gets the same Plane game and the update fix.

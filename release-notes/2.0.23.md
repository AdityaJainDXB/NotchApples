**New**
- ☕ **A coffee button beside the pin.** Click it to keep your Mac awake, as long as you like. Click it again to let it sleep. It's the same Keep Awake as in Tools, so the countdown beside the notch shows too.

**Changed**
- 🧰 **Tools is tidier.** The two Keep Awake cards are now **one**: a switch on top, and **Options** folds out the time limits (30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, Always) and the **Allow lid-closed running** switch. Tools shows three roomy cards instead of four.

**Fixed**
- 🔁 **Dragging a file onto the notch works for Convert.** If you were on the Convert tab, the notch now stays there instead of jumping to the Shelf. A file on the Shelf also has **Convert…** in its right-click menu, and the whole Convert tab accepts drops.

Windows 1.38.0 BETA has the same Convert drop fix, along with Convert, Klick and the Plane fixes.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

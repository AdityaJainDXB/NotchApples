[required-update]
**Required update.** Notch apple now enforces required updates properly.

- 🔒 **A required update can't be waited out.** Once Notch apple has seen that a release is required, it remembers it. Going offline or blocking the update server afterwards does not lift it.
- 🚫 **Until you update:** the notch shows only the **Update now** screen, paid features and most keyboard shortcuts are off (only opening and closing the notch still work), and notch badges stop. Settings and the menu bar still open so you can update or deactivate.
- 📦 Includes everything from 1.34.0 to 1.34.7, including the 10-minute license check and 3-day offline limit.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

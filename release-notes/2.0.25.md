**New**
- ⌨️ **Klick has a Mechanical sound.** The classic clacky keyboard: a sharp click as the key goes down, a hard plastic clack as it bottoms out, a ring from the case, and a crisp click on release. It's the first sound in the list in the Klick tab, with its own volume slider like the rest.

Windows 1.38.0 BETA gets it too, along with Convert, Klick and the Plane fixes.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

**New in the Plane game**
- 🛫 **Clean start**, on the home screen of the flight simulator. You spawn on the runway with the battery, beacon and engine off and do the whole start-up yourself: **Y** battery, **O** beacon, **S** throttle to idle, **I** engine, then flaps, gear and brake. The checklist on screen walks you through each step and ticks it off. With it off, you spawn ready to roll, as before.

**Security pass** (a full review of the app, the server and the website)
- The shared-flying relay is rate-limited per address, only shows pilots in the same part of the world, makes a fresh random id for every flight session, and everything it sends back is checked before it is drawn.
- The Bambu account sign-in only follows redirects within Bambu's own address, signs out completely (and tells you if it could not), and backs off when a printer keeps failing instead of reconnecting every few seconds.
- The autopilot hands back before it flies a height-hold into the ground, and a handful of flight-sim glitches are fixed (traffic left frozen after switching it off, the wrong checklist after landing in Landing mode, aircraft lit from the day sun at night).

Windows 1.64.0 BETA has the same changes.

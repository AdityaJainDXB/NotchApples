**New**
- 💣 **Air War, a new game mode in Plane.** Bomb tanks, trucks, bunkers, fuel depots and anti-aircraft guns, and shoot down enemy fighters, in waves that get harder. Fly with a computer wingman (Co-op) or with computer aces hunting you (Versus), on Easy, Normal or Hard.
- 🎯 **Real weapons.** Bombs fall with your speed and drag and drift in the wind, and a bombsight shows where one released now would land. Enter or a click fires the cannon, Z drops bombs (hold it for a stick), N fires a heat-seeking missile at the locked fighter, L fires flares. A radar shows enemies, your wingman and targets.
- ✈️ **Two new aircraft.** The B-52 Stratofortress heavy bomber carries 40 bombs and has tail gunners that shoot back. The A-10 Warthog gunship carries the most cannon ammunition and takes the most punishment. Every other aircraft can fight too.
- 🏅 Four new challenges.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch Apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

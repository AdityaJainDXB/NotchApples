[required-update]
**Required update.** This brings everyone onto the full 1.34 set of changes: the new Home with Classic and Widgets styles, Non-Necessities, the Claude usage tracker with history and accounts, PairDrop with private chat, and the guided tour.

**Licenses**
- 🔐 **A suspended or revoked key now stops working within hours, with no update needed.** Notch apple checks the signed revoked list at launch, after sleep and every hour (and no more often than every 6 hours). Before, it checked once a day and only at launch, so a Mac left running kept its access.
- ⏳ **A Mac that can't reach the server for 30 days pauses paid features** until it is online again, so blocking the server doesn't keep a suspended key alive. It comes back on its own once connected.

**Home**
- 🏠 A brand-new install starts on the **widget Home**. If you are updating, you keep the Classic page until you choose in Settings → Modules & Layout → Home screen.

Install: download the DMG below and drag Notch apple to Applications, or `brew upgrade --cask notch-apple`.

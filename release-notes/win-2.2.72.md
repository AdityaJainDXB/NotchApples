## Notch Apple for Windows 2.2.72 (BETA)

- New: liquid metal (recommended). Settings → Liquid metal gives the open tab, the notch rim and buttons a flowing metal look. It is off in Lite performance mode and with Reduce Motion
- New: desktop widgets (Ultimate). Clock, To-do and Now Playing as small windows on your desktop; drag them and Notch Apple remembers where you put them
- New: Humanizer. Tools → Humanizer rewrites text so it reads like a person wrote it, and every AI reply has a Humanize button. Free humanizes up to 500 words at a time and 3,000 a month, Pro 1,000 at a time and 15,000 a month, Ultimate any length and 40,000 a month. The words are counted on your own computer and renew on the 1st. Buying extra words is coming soon
- Fixed: Flight Economy opens with your Ultimate key, no code needed
- Fixed: in the F1 game the battery gauge no longer reads 0% on a 4:3 window (the tyre panel was drawn over it), and the telemetry panel no longer shows NaN kW before the race starts
- Fixed: in Racing, cars still on the road when you finish are no longer listed as DNF. The results now wait for them to cross the line (up to 30 seconds), and only a car that really retired shows DNF

Notch apple: read me first

1. Install

Drag Notch apple onto the Applications folder in this window, then open it from Applications.

2. If macOS says it can't verify the app

Notch apple is free and not notarized by Apple. The first time only: right-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again. Or open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

3. Turn on permissions (all optional)

The app opens Settings > Permissions on first launch. Click Allow next to what you want; macOS may ask you to confirm.

  • Screen Recording: needed for the AI's "what's on my screen?" and the Screenshot and Record buttons. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, switch Notch apple on, then click Relaunch in the app (macOS only applies it after a restart).
  • Accessibility: only for the Windows tab (snapping windows). Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
  • Calendars, Location, Notifications, Camera: for Today, weather, message alerts and face unlock.

If a switch looks on but a feature still says it needs permission, select Notch apple in the list, click the − button to remove it, open the app again and allow it once more.

4. Using it

Click the notch to open it. ⌘O hides or shows the notch anywhere. The gear button opens Settings. AI, Messenger, Audio, Now Playing and VPN need an access code; everything else is free.

Extra tabs, off until you switch them on in Settings > Modules: Browser (web and search from the notch), Launcher (open your favourite apps), Translator, Mac Stats, and more.
